Placing an order

An order is confirmed only when:

03

Pricing & payments

All prices on our site are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and include applicable taxes. We accept two payment options:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) — pay in cash when your order arrives
  • Advance Payment — bank transfer to HBL (Habib Bank Limited), with 10% off your subtotal

Delivery is Rs. 350, free on orders of Rs. 10,000 or more.

Advance Payment terms

When choosing Advance Payment:

  • The 10% discount applies to the subtotal, not the delivery fee
  • Delivery in 2–3 working days after verification
Fake or unverifiable transaction IDs will result in immediate cancellation.
