What We Collect & Why

The essentials

We only collect what's needed to get your order to your door — nothing more.

01

Information you give us

When you place an order, we ask for the details needed to deliver it safely:

  • Full name
  • Phone number (used for delivery confirmation)
  • Delivery address and city
  • Order notes (optional)
  • For Advance Payment: transaction ID and a screenshot of the bank transfer
02

How we use your information

Your data is used only for the following purposes:

  • Processing and dispatching your order
  • Sending order confirmations and delivery updates
  • Verifying Advance Payment transactions
  • Responding to customer service and returns
  • Meeting our legal and accounting obligations
We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.
03

Who can see your data

Only the people who need it to get your order to you:

  • Our in-house team — to pack and confirm your order
  • Our courier partner — receives only your name, phone and address
  • Our bank — for verifying Advance Payment transfers

We do not share your data with any other party.

04

How long we keep your information

We retain your order details for as long as required to:

  • Honour the 7-day returns window
  • Resolve any delivery issues or disputes
  • Meet tax and accounting record requirements

After that, your personal details are securely deleted.

05

Cookies & site basics

We use minimal browser storage to keep your shopping experience smooth:

  • Cart storage — your cart is saved locally in your browser so it doesn't disappear on refresh
  • No advertising cookies — we do not use tracking pixels or remarketing
  • No third-party analytics — we don't run Google Analytics or similar
06

Your rights

You are always in control of your information. You can:

  • Ask what data we hold about you
  • Request a correction to any detail
  • Request complete deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for further contact at any time

To exercise any of these, just email us — we respond within 24 hours.

07

Security

Your data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized team members. All payment screenshots you upload are handled with the same care as your order details and are never shared publicly.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no online system is completely immune to risk — if you ever suspect an issue with your account, contact us immediately.

08

Policy updates

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in how we operate or in the law. The latest version is always available on this page, and any material changes will be communicated via email to active customers.
