The Journey

From one sketch to every home

A quiet beginning, a lot of late nights, and a belief that Pakistani women deserved better everyday pieces.

2019

A studio, two machines, and a belief

Misk Official began in a small room in Lahore with two sewing machines, a hand-drawn sketchbook, and the simple idea that Pakistani women deserved everyday clothing that felt as considered as occasion wear.

2021

Our first full collection

The Basics edit launched with just six designs. Every piece sold out within a week. That was the moment we knew the approach was right — hand-embellished, honest fabrics, made in small batches.

2023

Building the atelier

We partnered with a small team of master karigars in Lahore — specialists in hand embroidery, gota work, and zardozi. Every Misk piece from that point on has been touched by real hands, not machines.

2024

Nationwide delivery

We expanded to deliver free across all of Pakistan, added Cash on Delivery and Advance Payment with a 10% discount, and set up real-person support on WhatsApp for every order.

Today

Five collections, one promise

Basics, Solids, Embroidered, Formals and Best Sellers — with new arrivals every Friday. Over 12,000 women across Pakistan have worn Misk. The promise hasn't changed: hand-finished, honest fabrics, made with care.

In the Atelier
Craftsmanship

Made slowly, made well

Every Misk piece passes through the hands of skilled Pakistani artisans — from pattern cutting to the final embroidery stitch. We work in small batches so each suit gets the attention it deserves, never rushed, never mass-produced.

  • Hand embroidery — done by master karigars, thread by thread.
  • Small batches — never mass-produced, never generic.
  • Designed in-house — every silhouette starts on our own sketch table.
  • Ethical making — fair pay for every artisan we work with.
